#### Findings
- [minor] `src/openhuman/agent_meetings/recent_calls.rs:55,60` — `.lock().unwrap()` on the registry mutex in `remember_join` and `take_join` deviates from the repo's no-`.unwrap()` convention for `src/openhuman/**`. Practically safe (mutex poisoning only happens after a prior thread panic while holding the lock), but `unwrap_or_else(|p| p.into_inner())` would recover rather than panic if poisoning ever occurs.
- [minor] `src/openhuman/agent_meetings/recent_calls.rs:122` — `now_ms.saturating_sub(duration_ms)` in the no-meta fallback silently produces a wrong `started_at_ms` when `duration_ms > now_ms` (e.g. clock at zero or artificially large duration in tests). A comment noting the precondition would help future readers; the existing test exercises the typical path but not this edge.

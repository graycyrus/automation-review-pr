## AI Summary
| Dimension | Rating | Detail |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| What it does | — | Adds a post-call meeting summarisation step to `agent_meetings`. After a Google Meet ends, a single LLM call produces a structured summary (headline + key points + action items) and a short context label. The label becomes the thread title and a secondary label alongside "Meetings"; the formatted summary is appended as a closing system message. Generation is best-effort — any failure falls back to the existing plain-transcript thread. |
| Breaking risk | Low | No public API, transport, or schema changes. `create_meeting_thread_with_transcript` signature is unchanged. The only new behaviour is an async LLM call on the non-critical post-call path. |
| Security risk | Zero | Transcript is passed as plain text to a config-resolved provider. No hardcoded credentials, no user-controlled URL resolution, no injection surface. Error messages logged at WARN level are low-risk (provider errors, not transcript content). |
| Bottom line | Safe to merge | Clean, well-tested backend-only feature with a sound best-effort design. |

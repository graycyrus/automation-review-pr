## AI Summary

| What it does | — | Fixes mid-session Composio integration staleness — when a user connects a toolkit (e.g. Notion OAuth) while a chat thread is open, the orchestrator's `delegate_to_integrations_agent` schema now refreshes in-thread without requiring a new chat or app restart. #### Findings
- [major] `src/openhuman/agent/harness/session/turn.rs:1355` — `synthesized_tool_names` stale state in partial-refresh path. When `tools_reconciled = false` (the normal case during `before_dispatch` since `AgentToolSource` always holds an Arc clone), `tool_specs` was updated to the new synthesized set but `synthesized_tool_names` was restored to `old_synth`. On the next connect, `old_synth` used as the removal mask didn't cover specs from the prior partial refresh — those survived the `retain` and got re-added by `extend`, duplicating them.
- [minor] `src/openhuman/memory_sync/composio/bus.rs:864,929` — slug normalization (`filter/sort/dedup`) duplicated verbatim in both subscribers. Extract to a helper.
- [minor] `src/openhuman/agent/harness/engine/tool_source.rs:111` — `sync_agent_surface` has 8 parameters plus `&mut self`. `#[allow(clippy::too_many_arguments)]` on trait default; consider a `SyncAgentSurfaceArgs` struct.
